Before you download

Is my iPhone compatible?

Plan2Real runs on iPhones that support augmented reality (ARKit), with iOS 17 or later. An iPhone Pro with a LiDAR sensor places the plan faster and more precisely, especially on difficult floors.

Which plans can I use?

A photo of a paper plan, an image from Photos or a PDF file. For a PDF, the real page size is read automatically.

Is it accurate?

It's a visualization tool, for guidance only. The screen shows the A–B dimension so you can check it with a tape, and aligning on two real landmarks corrects the plan on a large lot.

Does it work outdoors?

Yes, on a floor with texture (asphalt, raw concrete, short grass). Snow, water and glossy floors create reflections that confuse augmented reality.

Do I need an account?

No. No account, no ads, no trackers: your projects stay on your iPhone.

Which languages?

English and French, with an FR / EN button on the home screen.

What about Android?

Not at the moment: Plan2Real is for iPhone only.
